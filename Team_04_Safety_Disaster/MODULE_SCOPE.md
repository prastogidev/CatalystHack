# Team 04 — Module Scope & Modification Boundaries

## Allowed Modifications
- `services/crime_data.py`
- `services/disaster.py`
- `models/safety.py`, `models/disaster.py`
- `api/disaster.py`
- `data/crime/**`
- `data/disaster/**`
- `tests/test_safety_crime.py`, `tests/test_disaster.py`

## Strictly Forbidden
- Do NOT alter official NCRB 2022 crime statistics or invent baseline figures.
- Do NOT modify other teams' services (e.g. `services/scoring.py`).
